      About Plane Goes

      Where the flight data comes from

      • Live plane positions: adsb.lol, made available under the Open Database License (ODbL).
      • Flight routes: adsbdb.
      • Backup routes: hexdb.io.

      Thank you to the volunteers who feed and run these free services.

      Privacy

      Your location is read only when you tap Spot, to find nearby planes. The app never saves where you were standing.
